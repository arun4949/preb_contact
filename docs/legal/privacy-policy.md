This Privacy Policy explains how Kranz & Gupta, Indiepal GbR ("Preb", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit preb.co, create an account, use the Preb application, receive our emails or contact our support.

It is written for two groups of people:

- **Customers and Users:** the people and companies who create a Preb workspace, sign in and use the service.
- **Contacts:** the people whose details our Customers upload to Preb and ask us to enrich (for example candidates and hiring managers). If you are a Contact, Section 12 is written for you.

We are a German company. The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to everything we do, no matter where you live. Because most of our Customers are in the United States, Section 13 adds the information required by US state privacy laws.

## 2. Our two roles: controller and processor

Preb handles personal data in two different roles, and your rights depend on which one applies.

**Preb as controller.** We decide how and why to process the data of our Customers, Users, website visitors and support contacts. This covers your account, sign-in, billing, support conversations, product emails, security logs and cookies. Sections 3 to 11 describe this processing.

**Preb as processor.** When a Customer uploads a spreadsheet of Contacts and asks us to find work emails, personal emails or phone numbers, we act only on that Customer's documented instructions. The Customer is the controller of the Contact data and decides why it is processed. We do not use Contact data for our own purposes, we do not sell it, we do not combine Contact data across different Customers, and we do not build our own contact database from it. This processing is governed by our Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which forms part of our contract with every Customer and lists the categories of data, the retention periods, the security measures and our sub-processors.

If you are a Contact and want to know why a particular company processed your data, that company is your first point of contact. We will still help you, as described in Section 12.

## 3. The personal data we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service. The data falls into the following categories.

### 3.1 Account and profile data

When you sign up we collect your email address, your full name, the name of your workspace and the role you hold in it (owner, admin or member). If you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google profile picture and your Google account identifier. We record when you created your account, when you completed onboarding and when you were invited to a workspace.

Preb is a business tool. During sign-up we check the domain of your email address and do not accept addresses from free webmail providers or disposable email services, unless you were invited by an existing workspace. Invitations contain the invitee's email address and the role assigned by the inviter.

### 3.2 Sign-in and session data

Preb has no passwords. You sign in with a one-time magic link that we send to your email address, or with your Google account. We process the sign-in token, the time of sign-in and a session cookie that keeps you signed in. We limit the number of magic links per email address to protect against abuse.

If you use Google sign-in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use this information only to create and secure your account.

### 3.3 Billing data

Payments are handled by Stripe. When you buy a plan, Stripe collects your card details, billing address, an optional tax identification number and any promotion code you enter. We never see or store full card numbers. We store the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, your plan, your billing period, your subscription status and your credit grants and consumption, because we need them to run your workspace and to issue invoices.

### 3.4 Usage, device and log data

When you use preb.co, our hosting provider records technical data about each request: your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, the pages and endpoints you call, timestamps, referring page and error codes. Our authentication provider keeps sign-in logs. We use no error monitoring or session recording service.

Inside the application we keep records of what happens in your workspace: lists created, enrichment runs started, stopped or paused, credits consumed, members invited and plan changes. Some of these events appear in your notification center.

### 3.5 Support and feedback data

Our support chat, help center and feedback board are provided by Featurebase. When you open them while signed in, we pass a signed identity token so that the support conversation shows who is writing. The token contains your user ID, email address, name, profile picture, account creation date, role, and your workspace's name, plan, monthly plan price, available credits, trial status, subscription status and billing period end. The content of your messages, feature requests and votes is stored by Featurebase.

### 3.6 Communication data

We keep a log of every email we send you (recipient address, type of email, provider message ID and time). We use this log to avoid sending duplicate emails, to enforce rate limits on sign-in emails and to prove delivery if there is a dispute. If you write to us, we keep the correspondence.

### 3.7 Customer Content (processed on behalf of our Customers)

Customer Content is the Contact data our Customers upload and the results we return. It includes:

- the uploaded CSV or XLSX file and every column it contains, including columns we do not use for enrichment;
- the input fields mapped by the Customer: first name, last name, full name, company name, company domain, LinkedIn profile URL and email address;
- contacts entered manually one by one;
- the results of enrichment: work email addresses and their deliverability status, personal email addresses and their status, phone numbers with line type, region and ownership match, job title, current company, company domain and logo, location, and public professional profile data returned by our data provider, which can include headline, employment history, education, skills, languages and professional network identifiers;
- the identity of a person identified from an email address (reverse lookup).

Customer Content is only processed as described in Section 2 and in the DPA. Each workspace can see only its own Customer Content.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage entries.

**Strictly necessary.** These are required for the service to work and do not need your consent:

- Session cookies set by our authentication provider (names beginning with `sb-`) that keep you signed in and refresh your session.
- The consent cookie set by our consent manager, Termly, which remembers your cookie choices.
- Browser storage used by the support widget to keep your conversation open.
- Local storage entries that remember your light or dark mode choice and the columns you show in a table. These never leave your browser.

**Consent-based.** Analytics, measurement or marketing cookies are only set after you accept them in the consent banner. Our consent manager blocks such scripts until you decide. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the "Cookie preferences" link in the account menu and in the website footer. We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as an opt-out from non-essential cookies.

We currently use no analytics or marketing cookies at all. If we introduce any, we will list them in the Cookie Policy and set them only after your consent.

A full list of cookies with names, purposes and lifetimes is published in our Cookie Policy.

## 9. How we protect your data

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk:

- All traffic between your browser, our application and our providers is encrypted with TLS. Data is encrypted at rest at our database and storage provider.
- Each workspace's data is isolated by database-level row security. A user can only read data of workspaces they are a member of.
- Access within a workspace is role based. Only owners and admins can manage members, billing and delete lists.
- We use passwordless sign-in with expiring magic links or Google sign-in, so there are no passwords to steal.
- Incoming notifications from our data provider and from Stripe are checked with cryptographic signatures before we act on them.
- Uploaded files are stored in a private bucket and are only reachable through signed, time-limited links.
- Card data is handled by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified provider, and never touches our systems.
- Access to production systems is limited to the two founders and protected by two-factor authentication.
- We log security-relevant events and review errors and anomalies.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the competent supervisory authority as required by Art. 33 and 34 GDPR.

## 10. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the following rights with respect to the personal data we process as controller:

- **Access** (Art. 15): to know whether we process your data and to receive a copy.
- **Rectification** (Art. 16): to have inaccurate data corrected. You can change your name yourself in your profile settings.
- **Erasure** (Art. 17): to have your data deleted where we no longer need it or where you withdraw consent.
- **Restriction** (Art. 18): to have processing limited while a dispute about the data is resolved.
- **Portability** (Art. 20): to receive the data you provided to us in a machine-readable format. You can export your lists yourself at any time.
- **Objection** (Art. 21): to object to processing based on legitimate interest, including product news. We will stop unless we can show compelling legitimate grounds.
- **Withdrawal of consent** (Art. 7 (3)): where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future, for example through the cookie preferences.
- **Complaint** (Art. 77): to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place. The authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2-4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de.

## 11. How to exercise your rights and delete your account

Write to hello@preb.co from the email address linked to your account, or send a letter to the postal address in Section 1. Tell us which right you want to exercise. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by replying from your account email or by signing in. We answer within one month. If a request is complex we may extend this by up to two months and will tell you why.

**Deleting your account or workspace.** You can do both yourself in the application. Settings, Profile, "Delete account" removes your profile, your sign-in, your notifications and your memberships, and deletes every workspace you own together with all its lists, contacts, uploaded files and credits. Workspace owners can also delete only the workspace under Settings, Members, "Delete workspace". Both actions ask you to type your email address or the workspace name, take effect immediately and cannot be undone. A running subscription is cancelled at once, and credits already used are not refunded. Remaining team members are informed in the application. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. Billing records that we must keep under tax law are retained for the statutory period and are not used for anything else. Export your lists before deleting, because deletion is permanent. If you prefer, you can also email hello@preb.co and we delete the account for you within 30 days.

**Members and invitations.** Workspace owners and admins can remove members at any time, and members can leave a workspace themselves. Removing a member does not delete the lists they created, because those belong to the workspace.

Exercising your rights is free of charge. We will not discriminate against you for doing so.

## 12. Information for Contacts: people whose data our Customers enrich

If a recruiting team has used Preb to find your contact details, this section is for you.

**What happens.** A Customer uploads a spreadsheet or enters details of people they want to reach, typically name and employer, a LinkedIn profile URL or an email address. On the Customer's instruction we ask specialist B2B contact data providers to find and verify a work email address, a personal email address or a phone number, and to return public professional profile information such as job title, employer and location. The results are shown to the Customer and can be downloaded by them.

**Who is responsible.** The Customer who uploaded your details is the controller. It decides why it wants to contact you and must have a lawful basis for doing so. We act as its processor. We do not decide to look you up, we do not use your data for our own purposes, we do not combine your data across Customers, we do not sell it and we do not build a database of Contacts.

**Where the data comes from.** Your data comes from the Customer's own files and from our data providers, who compile publicly available professional information and verify contact details. The providers' own privacy notices explain their sources.

**How long it is kept.** We keep your data for the Customer for as long as the Customer keeps the list, and in a workspace-level cache for a maximum of 90 days. Our data providers keep results for 3 months. See Section 8.

**Your rights.** You can exercise your GDPR rights (Section 10) against the Customer. If you do not know who the Customer is, or you prefer to contact us, write to hello@preb.co. We will:

- tell you, if we can, which Customer processed your data, or forward your request to them;
- help the Customer respond to your request within the legal deadlines;
- on your request, add your email address, phone number or LinkedIn URL to our suppression list so that Preb will not return or display those details to any Customer in the future. We keep this list under our legitimate interest in honouring your objection (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). It contains only the data needed to recognise and suppress your details.

If you live in the United States, Section 13 also applies to you.

We do not make decisions about you that are based solely on automated processing and that have legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR).

If one of these checks blocks you incorrectly, email hello@preb.co and a founder will review it.
