This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") is part of the Terms of Service (the "Terms") between Kranz & Gupta, Indiepal GbR, Brunnenstr. 11, 53123 Bonn, Germany ("Preb") and the Customer. Capitalised terms have the meaning given in the Terms. "GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679. "Data Protection Law" means the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, the national laws implementing them, and the US state privacy laws listed in Section 13, in each case as applicable to the Customer Data. "Customer Data" in this DPA means the personal data in Customer Data and Results as defined in the Terms. "Sub-processor" means a third party engaged by Preb to process Customer Data.

**1.1** This DPA governs the processing of Customer Data by Preb. It does not govern Preb's processing of account, billing, support, website or usage data of the Customer and its Users, for which Preb is the controller and which is described in the Privacy Policy.

**1.3** This DPA starts with the Terms and remains in force until Preb has deleted all Customer Data in accordance with Section 10.

## Annex 1: Details of the processing

**Subject matter.** Enrichment and verification of contact data of candidates, hiring managers and other business contacts that the Customer uploads to or enters in the Preb application, and the storage, display and export of the results.

**Nature and purpose.** On the Customer's instruction Preb sends the inputs it has provided to its enrichment Sub-processor, which finds and verifies work email addresses, personal email addresses and phone numbers, identifies a person from an email address, and returns public professional profile data. Preb stores the results in the Customer's Workspace, shows them in the application, allows filtering and export, caches them per Workspace for reuse within 90 days, and deletes them on the Customer's instruction. The purpose is recruiting and hiring by the Customer, as permitted by the Terms.

**Categories of data subjects.** Candidates, hiring managers, recruiters, employees of target companies and other business contacts that the Customer wants to reach for recruiting purposes.

**Categories of personal data.**

- Identification data: first name, last name, full name.
- Professional data: current and past employer, company domain, job title, seniority, location, LinkedIn profile URL and identifiers, professional headline, employment history, education, skills and languages as returned by the enrichment Sub-processor.
- Contact data: work email address and its deliverability status, personal email address and its status, phone numbers with line type, region, ownership confidence and connect rate.
- Any other columns contained in the files the Customer uploads, which are stored unchanged and returned in exports.
- Technical data: row position, internal identifiers, timestamps, credits consumed per row.

**Special categories of personal data.** None. The Customer undertakes not to submit special categories of personal data, data relating to criminal convictions or data about children (Section 5.2).

**Duration.** For the term of the Terms. Retention periods are set out in Section 10 and in the Privacy Policy.

**Processing operations.** Collection (upload), storage, organisation, structuring, transmission to the enrichment Sub-processor, retrieval, consultation, use, alignment with cached results, restriction, erasure.

## Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

Preb implements the following measures, which it reviews at least once a year and after every significant change to the Service.

**Pseudonymisation and encryption.** All connections between users, the application and Sub-processors are encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest in the database, in file storage and in backups is encrypted by the hosting provider with AES-256. Deleted accounts are remembered only as a one-way hash of the email address for abuse prevention. Engine logs are structured and never contain contact data.

**Confidentiality.** Every Workspace's data is isolated by row level security policies in the database, so that a User can only read data of Workspaces they are a member of, and only the server-side service role can read across Workspaces. Within a Workspace, access is role based (Owner, Admin, Member). Service-role keys and provider API keys exist only on the server and in the hosting provider's encrypted environment configuration, never in the browser. Uploaded files are stored in a private bucket and are reachable only through signed, time-limited links. Access to production systems is limited to the two founders, protected by two-factor authentication at every provider, and reviewed when a person's role changes.

**Integrity.** Users sign in without passwords through expiring magic links or Google sign-in. Incoming notifications from Sub-processors and from the payment provider are verified with cryptographic signatures before they are processed and are recorded for idempotent processing. Credits and results are reconciled against the Sub-processor's authoritative response. Database changes are made through versioned migrations.

**Availability and resilience.** The application runs on a global hosting platform with automatic scaling and failover. The database provider takes daily backups and keeps them for a limited rotation period. Enrichment runs are resumable: an interrupted run continues where it stopped, and provider outages pause runs and retry automatically.

**Restoration.** Backups can be restored by the database provider on request. The founders test the restore procedure at least once a year.

**Testing and evaluation.** Automated tests run on every change. Type checks and linting are enforced. Security and performance advisories of the database provider are reviewed after every schema change. Sub-processors' SOC 2 reports are reviewed once a year.

**User identification and authorisation.** Each User has one account tied to one email address. Sessions are bound to signed tokens with limited lifetime and refreshed server-side. Invitations are single-use tokens that expire after 7 days. Rate limits apply to sign-in emails and invitations.

**Protection of data during transmission.** TLS for all traffic, HTTP Strict Transport Security on the application domain, signed webhooks, no transfer of contact data by email.

**Protection of data during storage.** Encryption at rest, private buckets, row level security, separation of Workspaces, no cross-Workspace reuse of results.

**Physical security.** Data centres of the hosting providers (AWS and DigitalOcean) are certified under SOC 2 and ISO 27001 with physical access controls. Preb operates no servers of its own.

**Event logging.** Request logs at the hosting provider, authentication logs at the database provider and structured engine logs allow security events to be traced. Logs contain no contact data. Retention follows the Privacy Policy.

**System configuration.** Infrastructure is defined in code and reviewed before deployment. Default-deny access policies in the database. Dependencies are kept current.

**Internal governance.** The two founders are jointly responsible for data protection. Privacy and security are part of every feature review. Incidents are handled according to Section 8.

**Certification and assurance.** Preb relies on the certifications of its Sub-processors (SOC 2 Type II at Supabase, Vercel and FullEnrich) and makes them available as described in Section 11.

**Data minimisation.** Only the fields selected by the Customer are requested from the enrichment Sub-processor. Only the inputs needed for enrichment are sent (names, company, domain, LinkedIn URL or email address). Error reports and logs exclude contact data.

**Data quality.** Results carry their verification status so that the Customer can judge their reliability. The Customer can correct or delete any record.

**Limited retention.** Lists until deletion by the Customer; cache 90 days; raw notifications 30 days; backups rotated within 30 days; daily automated cleanup jobs enforce these periods.

**Accountability.** Records of processing, this DPA, the Sub-processor List and the Privacy Policy are kept current and published.

**Portability and erasure.** Export of every List in CSV format at any time; self-service deletion of Lists, Workspaces and accounts; deletion cascades through all related records and files.

**Measures for Sub-processors.** Written data processing agreements with Standard Contractual Clauses or Data Privacy Framework certification, review of the Sub-processors' security documentation, and the chain transparency described in Section 6.5.
